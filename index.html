Ron Mosenzon

About Me

I'm a fifth-year Ph.D. student at the Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago (TTIC), advised by Prof. Julia Chuzhoy. My expected graduation date is Summer 2027.

I am interested in algorithms for central graph problems in a variety of settings, including fast classical algorithms, approximation algorithms, dynamic algorithms and more. Some of my work focuses on flow, cut and distance-based problems in graphs.

I got my B.Sc. in Computer Science at the Open University of Israel, and my M.Sc. in Computer Science at the Weizmann Institute of Science, where I was advised by Prof. Robert Krauthgamer.
